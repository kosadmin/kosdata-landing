What we do

Everything you need, in one place.

Three integrated services built to unlock the full potential of your training pipeline — from raw capture to a licensable dataset.

Data Collection

Field-ready capture across every viewpoint your model needs — filmed onsite, in-studio, or through our APAC collector network.

  • Up to 4K/60fps synced multi-camera capture
  • Real operating environments, not staged sets
  • Consent & compliance handled on-site

Data Annotation

A QA-approved labeling workflow across six modalities, tuned for the precision robotics and computer vision teams need.

  • Image, video, audio, text, 3D & document labeling
  • Multi-stage QA with tracked inter-annotator agreement
  • Delivered in COCO / YOLO / VOC-ready formats

AI Training Datasets

Pre-built or custom-commissioned datasets, documented and packaged so they can be licensed again and again.

  • Covers robotics, computer vision, speech & language
  • Ready-to-train HDF5/RLDS, COCO, YOLO, JSON exports
  • Full documentation on specs, consent & quality metrics

How we work

From brief to delivery in five steps.

A repeatable, quality-controlled structure that scales from a 500-hour pilot to a 50,000-hour production program.

1

Specification

Model requirements translated into a capture or annotation spec — configuration, task scripts, diversity matrix, delivery format.

2

Protocol Design

Hardware setup, guidelines, participant/consent flows and QA checklists built before execution starts.

3

Execution

Domain-trained operators and annotators run the program, with daily progress reports and sample batches for review.

4

QA Review

Multi-stage review by domain-trained QA engineers — consistency, quality, diversity and compliance.

5

Delivery

Dataset delivered in your preferred format, with documentation covering specs, consent and quality metrics.

Need only part of the process? Every stage above can stand alone — from workforce and location sourcing to production and final QA — KOS slots into wherever you need extra capacity, full pipeline or a single service.
